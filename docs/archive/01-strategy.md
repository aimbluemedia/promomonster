# 01 — Strategy

## 1. One-line positioning

> **PromoMonster — Real People. Real Answers. Real Traffic.**
> A managed panel of real people who tell you what they think of your website, your ad
> creative and your competitors — and who will go look at what you publish.

Two audiences, one panel, one brand.

**To businesses:** *"Find out what real people think of your site — and put it in front of
them. From $49/month."*

**To panelists:** *"Get paid to share your opinion. $8–$16/hour, on your own schedule,
no selling, no experience."*

Note the panelist promise is stated as an **hourly rate**, not "5¢ a task." Cents-per-task
framing recruits the wrong people (bonus hunters, fraud) and reads as a get-paid-to-click
site to every payment processor that looks at you. Hourly framing recruits panelists and
reads as market research.

## 2. What we actually sell

Ordered by gross profit contribution, not by how easy they are to explain.

### Tier 1 — Research (the profit engine)

| Product | What the buyer gets | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Site Feedback Study** | 50–500 real people visit your page and answer 3–6 questions. First impression, comprehension, objection, intent. | 12 credits/response (~$0.90–1.20) |
| **Head-to-Head Test** | Two headlines / hero images / offers / logos. Which wins, and *why*, in the respondent's words. | 15 credits/response |
| **Search Result Test** | Your listing against three real competitors' SERP snippets. Which would they click, and why — reviews, brand, headline, offer? | 15 credits/response |
| **Social Creative Test** | Real thumbnail + title + first three seconds, rendered in a feed frame on our own page. Would they click? What did they expect? Thumb-stop and 3-second recall variants. See [07](07-social-product-design.md). | 15 credits/response |
| **Awareness Tracking** | Same five brand-recall questions to a fresh sample monthly, trended. No platform contact, natural subscription product. | 12 credits/response |
| **Targeted Study** | Any of the above, filtered on profile: state, ZIP, age, income band, homeowner, B2B job function, category intent. | 30–60 credits/response |

This is the category with real comparables — PickFu, Wynter, UserTesting, Prolific — where
buyers already pay $1.50–$3.00+ per response and don't blink. We enter under that and
still hold 60–70% margin.

### Tier 2 — Traffic (the acquisition wedge)

| Product | Requirement | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Verified Visit** | PromoMonster tag installed. 60s+ dwell, scroll depth reported. | 2 credits (~$0.15–0.20) |
| **Targeted Visit** | Tag + geo/device filter. | 3 credits |
| **Content Read** | Tag + 2-minute dwell + comprehension check. | 4 credits |
| **Unverified Visit** | No tag. Best-effort, sold as such, no delivery guarantee. | 1.5 credits |

Traffic is how a $49/month local business becomes a customer. It is not how you get rich.
Sell it honestly: *exposure and awareness from real humans*, never *conversions* and never
*rankings*.

### Tier 3 — Leads (Phase 3, email-only)

After a relevant study, panelists may opt in to be contacted by that business. Explicit,
granular, unbundled consent with a stored consent record. **Email address only at launch —
no phone numbers.** Phone capture drags you into TCPA and state mini-TCPA exposure that a
seed-stage company should not be carrying. $2–$25 per lead by category.

### Explicitly not sold, ever

Search ranking manipulation · social likes/follows/subscribes/upvotes · ad clicks ·
reviews or testimonials of any kind · app installs for rank · account creation ·
anything on a site monetized by an ad network.

Each of these is in the Acceptable Use Policy and each has a corresponding automated
check. See [05-risk-compliance.md](05-risk-compliance.md).

## 3. Why the draft's product mix was the wrong mix

Three of the six proposed products push risk onto the customer:

- **Paid social views** → your customer's YouTube channel absorbs the fake-engagement
  strike. Their view count gets scrubbed, and it can escalate to channel action. The
  "we only ask for a view, not a like" distinction is not one the platform makes.
- **Paid SERP clicks** → your customer's domain absorbs the manual action.
- **Paid traffic to a monetized site** → your customer's AdSense account absorbs the
  invalid-traffic termination.

You cannot build a durable SMB brand on products whose worst case is *"we destroyed the
asset you hired us to promote."* One support ticket like that, publicly posted, prices in
forever. The replacements (creative testing, SERP testing) deliver the same underlying
insight, carry none of the exposure, and command 5–10x the price.

## 4. Competitive frame

| | Who they are | Price point | Where we win |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PickFu** | Split-testing polls, general + targeted panel | ~$1.50–3.00/response | Price entry point; visit-the-live-site studies, not just image polls |
| **UserTesting / Userlytics** | Recorded video sessions | $50–$100+/session | Order of magnitude cheaper; SMB-affordable; n=200 not n=5 |
| **Wynter** | B2B message testing | $$$, enterprise | SMB and local-services market they ignore |
| **Prolific** | Academic/research participant pool | Participant pay + ~33% fee | Turnkey studies, not raw recruitment; business-buyer UX |
| **SerpClix** | Paid SERP clicks | ~$0.30–0.60/click | We don't sell the liability; we sell the insight |
| **Fiverr traffic sellers** | Bot traffic | ~$0.001/visit | Verified humans, tag-proven |

*Verify all competitor pricing before it appears in external-facing material — these are
directional figures for planning.*

The gap we are aimed at is real and unserved: **an SMB or agency that wants 200 real
opinions for under $200 and has it back tomorrow.** PickFu is closest and is beatable on
price, on the live-site format, and on the local/geo targeting that a pool built off
US local-business channels gives us.

## 5. Ideal customer profile (in priority order)

1. **Marketing agencies and freelance web designers** — repeat buyers, testing every client
   site, refer other agencies, and expense $150–500/month without a procurement process.
   Highest LTV, lowest CAC. Sell them white-label reports.
2. **Local service businesses** (home services, dental, legal, pool, HVAC) — reachable
   through SearchMonster and MonsterList, and they understand "what do people think of my
   website."
3. **Ecommerce and Amazon sellers** — the PickFu core. Buy head-to-head tests constantly.
4. **Bloggers, creators, affiliates** — buy traffic and creative tests. Highest fraud/abuse
   rate; screen carefully.
5. **Early-stage SaaS** — landing-page message testing. Small segment, good logos.

Agencies first. One agency is worth twenty local businesses and brings its own distribution.

## 6. Go to market

**Phase 0 — concierge (weeks 1–10).** No self-serve. Recruit 400–800 US panelists. Sell 20
studies by hand — DM agencies on Reddit (r/PPC, r/SEO, r/marketing, r/juststart),
LinkedIn outreach, your existing SearchMonster/MonsterList customer list. Fulfil manually.
The output is a written list of *what buyers actually asked for*, which is the real spec.

**Phase 1 — self-serve research (weeks 10–22).** Ship the MVP in
[03-product-spec.md](03-product-spec.md). Convert the concierge customers.

**Phase 2 — traffic + ecosystem (months 6–9).** Ship the tag, verified visits, and the
in-product upsells inside SearchMonster / MonsterList / ContentVendor. This is genuinely
strong distribution and costs nothing per acquisition — the draft was right about it.

**Phase 3 — scale (months 9–18).** Targeted panel, API, agency/white-label accounts,
browser extension, leads.

### Acquisition channels, ranked by expected efficiency

1. **Your own ecosystem** — "See what real people think of your listing" inside
   SearchMonster and MonsterList; "Test your new article on 100 readers" inside
   ContentVendor. Near-zero CAC, warm audience.
2. **Agency partner program** — 20% recurring for 12 months. Agencies resell.
3. **Programmatic SEO landing pages** — one per use case *and* per city/vertical:
   `/website-feedback`, `/landing-page-testing`, `/headline-testing`,
   `/amazon-listing-testing`, `/website-feedback-for-dentists`. Long tail, high intent.
4. **Free public research as content marketing** — this is the highest-leverage and least
   obvious channel. Run studies on your own panel and publish them: *"We asked 500 people
   which of these 20 dentist websites they'd trust. Here's what won."* It's link bait, it
   demonstrates the product, it costs you ~$175 of panel time, and it recruits panelists
   and customers in the same asset. Do one every two weeks.
5. Reddit/Quora participation, cold email to agencies, YouTube walkthroughs.

### Panelist acquisition

Target 3,000 active US panelists by month 12 — not 50,000. Sources: referral (5% of
referee earnings for 90 days), Reddit beermoney communities (be honest there — they'll
audit you and their verdict is durable either way), Facebook side-hustle groups, and
survey-aggregator listings. Budget $2.50–4.00 per activated panelist.

**Recruit against demand, not ahead of it.** A panelist who signs up and finds an empty
task feed is gone permanently and will say so publicly. Keep a waitlist and open it as
campaign volume rises. Target: median panelist sees ≥ 5 available tasks at any time.

## 7. Roadmap

Sequenced for a solo founder on under $25k — see [00-execution-plan.md](00-execution-plan.md)
for the week-by-week version, budget and kill criteria.

**Phase 0 — Concierge (weeks 1–10). Almost no code.**
Landing pages, a waitlist, Tally forms, Airtable, PayPal Mass Payout, Stripe Payment Links.
Studies fielded and delivered by hand. Target: **20 paid studies, 400 panelists.** The
questions buyers ask here are the real specification. Gate: don't build until 20 studies
are sold and 8+ buyers have repeated.

**Phase 1 — Minimum self-serve (months 3–8, ~400 hours solo).**
Auth, panelist signup/profile/feed/task-runner/payouts, business signup, Stripe credit
packs, templated study builder, live results, CSV export, admin queues, ledger, task
leasing, fraud v1. **One task type** (Site Feedback; Head-to-Head is the same engine with
two URLs). Subscriptions, trust score, referrals, PDF reports and LLM summaries are all
deliberately cut — see [00-execution-plan.md](00-execution-plan.md) §4.

**Phase 2 — Months 8–12.**
Subscriptions, trust score, referrals, white-label PDF reports, automated payouts, basic
profile targeting. Driven by Phase 1 customer requests, not by this list.

**Year two.**
Traffic products and the PromoMonster tag, ecosystem upsells inside SearchMonster /
MonsterList / ContentVendor, agency and white-label accounts, API, Leads (email-only),
targeted panel at scale.

**Deliberately deferred past year one:** the browser extension (research tasks don't need
it), international panel, mobile apps, the AI study builder, and any revisit of paid
social or search products — see [06-platform-enforcement.md](06-platform-enforcement.md) §7.

## 8. Long-term

The durable asset is not the software. It's **a profiled, verified, retained panel of real
US consumers who answer honestly and can be sliced by geography and demographic.** Software
is copyable in a quarter; a panel with three years of behavioral history and a 40% monthly
retention rate is not.

Everything else compounds off that: research → creative testing → brand tracking → consumer
insight subscriptions → an opt-in audience businesses can reach on demand. That is a
company. "Buy clicks" is a side hustle with a compliance problem.
